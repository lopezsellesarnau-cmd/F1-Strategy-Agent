F1 Strategy Agent

Lap-time model & pit strategy simulation

A RandomForest model predicts lap time from tyre compound, tyre wear and lap number — trained on two Grand Prix, evaluated on a third it never saw. The result feeds a pit-stop strategy simulation on the test circuit.

Pit strategy simulation — Miami 2023, 57 laps

Assumes the circuit's baseline pace is known ahead of time (e.g. from practice/qualifying — here taken from the real race: 92.77s). The model only predicts how far each lap drifts from that pace, based on compound and tyre wear. Each car laps the track once, at a speed proportional to its simulated total race time — first one home wins.
